## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-07

### Added

- Exact cross projections with full nonunique families.
- Bilinear level-set projections for positive, zero, and negative levels.
- Canonical and bilinear-coordinate hyperbolic paraboloid projections.
- Bracketed monotone root solver and convergence metadata.
- In-place projection interfaces.
- Documentation, examples, tests, CI, Aqua, and code coverage configuration.
- Archived the repository's earlier experimental NMF scripts under `legacy/`.
