# Roadmap

## 0.1

- Cross, hyperbola, and rectangular hyperbolic paraboloid projectors.
- Complete exceptional branches and set-valued results.
- Float32, Float64, and BigFloat support.
- Documentation, CI, tests, and benchmarks.

## 0.2

- Batched projections.
- StaticArrays-focused methods.
- Allocation-reduced workspaces for repeated root solves.
- Performance baselines against generic constrained optimization.

## 0.3

- ChainRules extension on smooth singleton regions.
- Optional ProximalCore adapter.
- GPU compatibility audit.

## Later

- Structured bilinear operators and matrix-product constraints.
- Projection-based optimization examples: alternating projections,
  Douglas--Rachford, RRR, and consensus/product-space formulations.
